Studio Overview

Infinite Adrenaline is a founder-led studio specializing in competitive multiplayer systems. The founder is a gameplay systems programmer who built the current prototype in Unity (combat, inventory, and Mirror-based networking) — single-handedly. The studio's philosophy is to combine the immediate mechanical clarity of legacy shooters with modern systems and live-service growth mechanics so that a lean team can build a long-lived competitive IP.

Pre-Nexus Trials — Project Overview

PreNexus Trials is a competitive 2.5D PvP shooter built for PC. The game centers on faction-based warfare: teams fight in strategic skirmishes and large warzone battles while managing resources, inventory and unique faction abilities. Design goals are high-skill ceiling, high readability (for viewers), and tactical depth via inventory and ability interactions.
  • Dedicated server architecture using Unity + Mirror (authoritative sessions).
  • Faction-based play: humans, synthetics and other factions with distinct passives & ultimates.
  • Real-time inventory decisions: inventory is a tactical tool, not a menu pause.
  • Modes scale from small technical matches to large 50v50 warzones designed for spectacle and creator moments.

The Inventory: The True Heart of PreNexus Trials

In PreNexus Trials, the inventory is the hardest, most decisive tool in the game. It doesn’t just influence gameplay—it defines it, shaping speed, strategy, and every encounter. Without the right gear, rushing into battle is deadly; with careful planning, a single player can control the flow of combat.
Flexibility sets PreNexus Trials apart from traditional multiplayer FPS shooters that usually limit you carry to 2 or 3 weapons. You can become like the Doom Slayer, carrying 5–10 weapons and dominating in firepower. Or a grenade goblin, lurking in corners, controlling rooms with explosive precision, eliminating or smoking out enemy out of cover. Perhaps you’ll play a field medic, staying out of sight, reviving teammates, and keeping your faction in the fight.
The conventional path is always an option. You can equip yourself as a balanced unit with a bit of everything. But every item has a cost: space is the ultimate currency. One slot per item means that the choices you make are meaningful. Do you want heavy firepower, or extra health and grenades? Specialize, and you excel at that role; try to be an all-rounder, and you’ll be competent but never unmatched. Every match is a careful dance of planning, adaptation, and risk vs reward.
In PreNexus Trials, the inventory is the game—master it, and you control not just what you do, but how the battle unfolds. Your choices dictate the pace, suspense, and outcome of every encounter.

Development Roadmap

We deliver the product in milestone-linked tranches so investors can gate funding based on measurable outcomes. Each milestone has a clear deliverable and a KPI that signals readiness for the next tranche.

Phase 1 — Core Systems & Assets (0–6 months)

  • Complete first set of proprietary characters and weapon assets (replace temporary assets).
  • Polish inventory UI/UX and VFX; finalize one core map for skirmish mode.
  • Authoritative server stress tests and matchmaking prototype with Mirror; closed alpha with 200–500 testers.
  • KPI: Closed alpha retention ≥ 25%; netcode latency targets < 80ms.

Phase 2 — Open Beta & Creator Seeding (6–18 months)

  • Creator kit + trailer production and staged creator invitations.
  • Open Beta (Early Access) on PC (Steam Early Access target) with analytics instrumentation.
  • Prototype monetization test (cosmetics & season pass prototype).
  • KPI: Steam wishlist target 25k+; DAU/MAU ≥ 12%.

Phase 3 — Full Launch & Scale (18–36 months)

  • Full launch, live operations cadence, esports on-ramp via invitational events.
  • Regional marketing and partnerships; publisher conversations for Series A if needed.
  • KPI: sustainable recurring revenue by year 3 / validated monetization metrics.
